Spec Q&A

Common questions

Q01How long does it take to get my pack?

Usually 2–4 minutes after payment. You'll see a live progress indicator on the confirmation page.

Q02Do I need any technical skills?

None at all. Fill out the form, pay, and your pack is ready to download. The captions are written and ready to copy-paste.

Q03What platforms does it cover?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Twitter/X, and LinkedIn. You choose which ones matter for your business during checkout.

Q04Can I request revisions?

Each pack is a one-time generation. If you're not happy, email us and we'll work something out. We stand behind the quality.

Q05Is this only for New Orleans businesses?

We specialize in NOLA and the Gulf Coast, but any local business can use it. Just enter your city and your pack is tailored to your market.

Q06I rent a chair / don't own the shop. Is this for me?

Yes — that's exactly who Content Packs are built for. Tell us what you do (e.g. "independent barber, I rent a chair") and your pack is written in your own voice, about your work and your book, not the shop's. Your following stays yours wherever you go.

Q07What's the website in the Agency pack?

A fully coded single-page HTML website — header, hero, services, contact form, and footer — styled with your business colors and info. Download it and host it anywhere.
